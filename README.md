# 🛍️ Customer Analysis for Retail Store

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail store transaction data to uncover valuable business insights related to customer behavior, product performance, sales trends, and revenue generation.

Using Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), the project identifies purchasing patterns across different demographics, store types, and product categories to help businesses make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The primary objective of this project is to:

* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Identify top-performing product categories
* Analyze revenue trends and sales performance
* Discover demographic-based buying patterns
* Generate actionable business insights from transaction data

---

## 📂 Dataset Information

The analysis is performed using three different datasets:

### 👥 Customer Dataset

Contains customer demographic details such as:

* Customer ID
* Gender
* Date of Birth (DOB)
* City Code

### 🛒 Product Dataset

Includes product hierarchy information:

* Product Category
* Sub-Category

### 💳 Transaction Dataset

Contains retail transaction details such as:

* Transaction Date
* Quantity Purchased
* Price
* Tax
* Total Amount
* Store Type

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

## 📅 Transaction Timeline

Analyzed the complete transaction period to understand sales activity trends over time.

## 👨‍👩‍👧 Gender-Based Purchasing Patterns

Observed different product preferences among male and female customers across categories.

## 🌆 Customer Distribution by City

Identified the city contributing the highest number of customers.

## 🏬 Store Performance Analysis

The **E-Shop** channel generated:

* Highest revenue
* Maximum quantity sales

making it the best-performing store type.

## 💰 Revenue Contribution by Categories

Categories like:

* Electronics
* Clothing

contributed significantly to overall business revenue.

## 🔁 High-Value Customers

Identified customers who performed more than 10 transactions, indicating strong customer engagement and loyalty.

## 👨‍💼 Age Group Analysis (25–35 Years)

Customers aged between **25–35 years** showed high spending behavior, especially in:

* Electronics
* Books

## 📈 Seasonal Spending Trends

Analyzed customer spending trends between **January – March 2014** to identify seasonal purchase behavior.

## ✅ Conclusion

The analysis reveals that customer purchasing behavior varies significantly across demographics, store types, and product categories.

Key findings suggest that:

* E-commerce platforms are major revenue drivers
* Electronics and Clothing dominate sales contribution
* Young adult customers contribute heavily to spending
* Customer segmentation can help improve targeted marketing strategies

These insights can help retail businesses optimize operations, improve customer targeting, and increase profitability.
